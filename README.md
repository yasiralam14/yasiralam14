# Hi, I'm Yasir Alam 

I am a Computer Science researcher and Machine Learning Engineer specializing in Generative AI. My work bridges spatial computing, continuous latent representations, and advanced language model alignment.

## 🔬 Core Focus Areas

* **3D Computer Vision & Novel View Synthesis (NVS):** Researching 3D reconstruction architectures. I focus on evaluating and improving 3D Gaussian Splatting and non-Gaussian primitives, particularly regarding their structural integrity at high-frequency geometric edges and depth discontinuities.
* **Energy-Based Models (EBMs) & GenAI:** Developing continuous latent variable models and non-autoregressive generative frameworks. I actively build systems that replace standard priors with expressive EBMs trained via Contrastive Divergence and Langevin MCMC.
* **LLM Alignment & Multi-Agent Systems:** Fine-tuning language models using Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (GDPO/GRPO). I am currently designing state-tracking multi-agent architectures capable of continuous reasoning and infinite context ingestion.

## 🚀 Highlighted Work

* **Discontinuity-Aware NVS Evaluation:** My Master's thesis framework utilizing Laplacian pyramid zoning to quantify 3D rendering accuracy at sharp boundaries.
* **EBM Priors for Latent Generation:** A PyTorch implementation learning EBM priors over the latent space of generative models.
* **State-Tracking Agentic LLMs:** A Llama-3.1-8B model aligned via SFT and GDPO to conduct structured, multi-turn interviews while maintaining a dynamic internal reasoning state.
* **Hierarchical VAEs (HT-HVAE):** A two-level hierarchical architecture combining DistilBERT and GPT-2 for coherent, long-form text generation.
